Why Choose Right Strike Martial Arts Club

Right Strike Martial Arts Club offers authentic martial arts training that builds confidence, discipline, and a strong sense of community.

Our approach blends traditional Shito‑Ryu principles with modern coaching methods, ensuring safety, personal growth, and a supportive environment for every student.

Founder & Coaches

"Karate is not just about learning how to fight; it's about learning how to master yourself. My goal is to build martial artists who are strong not just in the dojo, but in every aspect of life."
Neeraj Founder & Head Coach

Neeraj is the visionary founder of Right Strike Martial Arts Club, bringing years of dedicated practice and teaching in Shito-Ryu Karate. His passion for the art goes beyond techniques — he believes martial arts transforms lives, building not just stronger bodies but stronger characters and sharper minds in every student he trains.

"A dojo is more than a training hall — it is a place where resilience is forged, discipline is cultivated, and every student discovers the champion within themselves."
Vigneshwar Ram Co-Founder

Vigneshwar Ram co-founded Right Strike Martial Arts Club with a mission to make quality martial arts education accessible to all. With a strong background in sports management and community building, he drives the club's vision of nurturing discipline, teamwork, and excellence — both on and off the mat.

"Every kick, every kata, every breath of effort in this dojo is a step toward a better version of yourself. I am here to guide that journey with patience, precision, and purpose."
Amjed Coach

Coach Amjed brings energy, precision, and deep technical knowledge to every class at Right Strike. Specialising in Shito-Ryu fundamentals and youth development, he has a natural ability to connect with students of all ages — making complex techniques approachable, fun, and effective for both beginners and advanced practitioners.
